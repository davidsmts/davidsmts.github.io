AI Security & Safety Research

David Schmotz

Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems · ELLIS Institute Tübingen · Tübingen AI Center

I work on AI Security and Safety. Most of my research is offensive: I look for the attack surfaces that appear once a model can read untrusted files, call tools, and hide its own reasoning, and I measure how badly frontier systems fail against them.

The goal is defensive. Attacks that are cheap to run and easy to reproduce are the ones that get fixed, so I try to build benchmarks and demonstrations that make a vulnerability hard to ignore.

Research

  1. Agentic AI security

    How agents that load third-party skills, read repositories, and execute shell commands can be attacked through the untrusted content they process.

  2. AI control & monitoring

    Whether weaker but trusted monitors can catch a capable model attempting hidden sabotage.

  3. Automated attacks with RL

    Using reinforcement learning to search for exploits automatically, instead of relying on hand-crafted jailbreaks or prompt injections.

  4. Distillation through model APIs

    How much of a model can be copied through its API, whether from the answers it returns or from what the interface leaks around them.
